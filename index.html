Eight screens, one scroll

Everything a lifter needs, and nothing that gets in the way.

01 · Home

Your week, in three rings.

Days, volume and sets close as you train. Set a weekly goal, keep the streak, and every session sits below as a card.

02 · Live workout

Two taps to log a set.

Last time's weight and reps are already in the row. Tick the set, the rest timer starts, and the next one is waiting.

03 · Routines

Routines that run themselves.

Folders, splits and programmes built in. Start a routine and every target is in place before you touch a plate.

04 · Progress

Every lift, charted.

Heaviest weight, estimated one-rep max, session volume. Pick a range and read the line. Records are flagged the moment they land.

05 · Calendar

Streaks you can see.

Trained days light up, rest days stay quiet. Flip between a month and a whole year of dots.

06 · Statistics

Numbers you can trust.

Sets per muscle, muscle distribution and monthly reports, all derived from the sets you logged and nothing else.

07 · Strength ranks

Rookie to Immortal.

Rank any lift against blended strength standards and climb the Mastery ladder one tier at a time.

08 · Achievements

Badges you earned.

Streaks, milestones and records fill a trophy case. No participation trophies.
